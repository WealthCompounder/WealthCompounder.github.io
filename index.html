Why Compounding

Time in the market does the heavy lifting — we just keep the ledger honest.

Compounding rewards consistency and patience far more than it rewards timing. Three habits matter more than any single fund choice.

START EARLY

Every year of delay is a year of growth you can't buy back

The earliest contributions have the longest runway to compound — starting sooner matters more than starting big.

STAY CONSISTENT

SIPs turn market noise into a routine

Regular, disciplined investing smooths out volatility over time rather than trying to predict it.

STAY INVESTED

Withdrawals need a plan, not a reaction

How and when you draw down a corpus matters as much as how you built it — especially in the early years of withdrawal.
